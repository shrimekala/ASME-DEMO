Why Join UH-ASME?

Joining the University of Houston's ASME chapter offers unparalleled opportunities for academic, professional, and personal growth. Connect with peers, faculty, and industry leaders, gain hands-on experience through projects, and enhance your resume with valuable skills and networking.

Learn More About Us

How to Join

Becoming a member of UH-ASME is a simple two-step process.

Step 1: Make account?

Become a member of the national American Society of Mechanical Engineers organization. This provides you with access to a global network, exclusive resources, and eligibility for national scholarships.

Sign Up

Step 2: Pay? or other way around?

Once you're a national member, join our local chapter through the University of Houston's "Get Involved" platform. This ensures you receive all local communications, event invitations, and member benefits.

Join on Get Involved

What to Expect After Joining:

After completing both steps, you'll be added to our mailing list and Discord server. You'll start receiving updates on general meetings, workshops, social events, and professional development opportunities. We look forward to seeing you at our next event!
