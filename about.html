What We Do

The American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) at the University of Houston is a professional student organization dedicated to fostering the growth of aspiring engineers. We connect students with industry professionals, provide hands-on project experience, and build a supportive community for academic and personal success.

Our Pillars

Our activities and community are built on these core principles.

Academic & Professional Growth

Gain an edge with workshops, company info sessions, and networking events.

Innovation & Teamwork

Apply your skills in hands-on projects and design competitions like our annual CAD Competition.

Outreach & Community Service

Give back to the Houston community and inspire the next generation of engineers through STEM outreach.

Supportive Community

Join a network of peers and mentors. We're more than a club; we're a family.

Membership Benefits

National Benefits

  • Access to the ASME Digital Collection and online journals.
  • Discounts on conferences, courses, and publications.
  • Eligibility for scholarships and grants.
  • Networking opportunities with a global community of engineers.

Local UH Chapter Benefits

  • Free access to our 3D printing services.
  • Exclusive workshops and company info sessions.
  • Mentorship from upperclassmen and alumni.
  • Free food at general meetings!
